# Current state and immediate focus — Ashen Reach

Updated 2026-09-28 (local). Read this file first. Follow the latest user request; historical milestone documents are not an active task queue.

## Immediate focus: customizable characters and scalable crowds

The user selected a long-term direction of customizable Human characters, existing Undead/Orc and future Elf support, 30 mixable armor sets, recurring quarterly PvP tiers, and crowded MMO hubs. The recommended architecture is authored/generated source art converted into compatible templates, procedural fitting/assembly, and progressively cheaper crowd representations.

**M001–M005 are complete; M006 is open on source art; M007’s semantic coverage and active Undead bind correction are verified, but the full fit milestone remains open.** The [M001 result](plans/character-mmo/results/m001.md) records the active asset census, fresh baseline and live motion. The [M002 result](plans/character-mmo/results/m002.md) records the pure versioned appearance recipe and seeded fixtures. The [M003 result](plans/character-mmo/results/m003.md) records Babylon Lite VAT/thin-instance capacity, mixed-outfit batching, churn and a reviewed [live clip](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m003-motion-2026-09-27.mp4) (Telegram **789**). Through M003 the Pages play output remained byte-identical. The [active Undead correction](plans/character-mmo/results/m007-undead-rebind.md) closes the rest/inverse-bind defect M001 found in all eight garments. Do not resume old graphics or world-expansion milestone queues.

The [M004 result](plans/character-mmo/results/m004.md) records the canonical Human template. The current authored Human is kept and a template derived from it: two morph targets, `slender` and `stout`, over the body the game already loads, with the same 3,274 vertices in the same order, the same 65-joint bind and the same 57 clips. Weight 0 is the shipped character in every semantic accessor, proved by hashing the geometry and the bind rather than the file. Girth ratios were measured from the vendored CC0 MakeHuman weight/muscle axis and applied in bone space, so joint centres, stature and arm span do not move. Uniform height reuses the existing `player.setHeightScale` clamp of **[0.9, 1.15]**, giving 1.58–2.02 m of visual stature against a 1.57–2.01 m Havok capsule; all five routes were walked at both endpoints with zero recoveries. A paired baseline/candidate measurement on the five routes showed **180.6–235.0 mean FPS** against a **180.3–234.6** baseline, within two tenths of a per cent on mean frame time, with identical draw submissions. The candidate GLB lives under `.cache/` and is reached only through an explicit query; the shipped character, the startup pack and the Pages payload are unchanged. Reviewed [live motion](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m004-shape-family-2026-09-27.mp4) is Telegram **790**.

M004 left one measured consequence, which M005 closed: the starter garments did not follow the shape. On the stout body, **364 of the 913** body vertices the Wayfarer tunic covers end up outside it and **250 of 756** under the trousers, worst new gap 30.2 mm; the slender body recedes far enough that the cloth floats. It also found a Lite 1.31.1 defect — the glTF morph feature ignores `bufferView.byteStride`, so an interleaved morph target renders as shattered geometry — worked around by writing the candidate's vertex data non-interleaved, with a test that fails if that ever regresses. Unlike M003's native baked-animation path, a morphed skinned body composes cleanly in the town's custom cascaded-shadow caster.

The [M005 result](plans/character-mmo/results/m005.md) closes the garment consequence M004 measured. All eight Human garments now carry the body's two shape targets in the same order, so one weight vector drives the whole outfit, and each garment vertex moves by the body displacement beneath it rather than by its own, which preserves the authored standoff in both directions. Across the tunic, trousers and boots the body vertices left outside their garment fall from **677 to 33**, with the neutral control at 0 in both; the 33 that remain are all at hems, where a heavier silhouette runs past where the garment ends, and adding material there is authoring rather than fitting. Applying the girth field to each garment's own offsets instead scored 99 and made the slender trousers worse than doing nothing; both rules are retained so the comparison can be rerun.

The catalogue had no rigid element, so M005 authored a shoulder-pauldron prototype on the same rig, every vertex weighted 1.0 to a single bone. Each pauldron is rigid through every pose of every clip by construction, and under the fit it stays a pure similarity — the same plate at a different size — within **0.0001 mm** of a uniform scale, against 16–34 mm for the cloth control. Two earlier fitting rules were rejected on evidence: one translation for the whole piece sank the plates into the stout shoulder, and one similarity for the whole mesh could only express what a left and a right pauldron share. Live editing costs about **0.012 ms** per outfit weight update and **0.611 MiB** of resident deltas, against **0.3 ms** and **313 KiB** to bake and re-upload a committed shape; the trade inverts with population, which M009 owns. A hem pass then stretches the last band of each hem inside the shape target, taking the residual from 33 to **23** and halving the stout trousers; the 23 that survive its 45 mm cap need authored material rather than more stretch, which is now measured rather than asserted. The plate has no catalogue slot — none of the seven is a shoulder — so `?plate=1` loads a body variant whose skin also drives it, which is not a slot system and gives it live motion. Reviewed live motion is Telegram **791** for the fit and [**792**](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m005-hems-and-plate-2026-09-28.mp4) for the hems and the plate. The refitted pack and the plate are developer candidates under `.cache/`, reached only through an explicit query; the shipped garments, the catalogue and the Pages payload are unchanged.

The [M006 gate result](plans/character-mmo/results/m006-gate.md) stops the creator slice before it starts, for measured reasons. The milestone needs height, body shape, adult age, skin and hair colour, and long hair versus bald. Height and body shape are delivered. **Bald is impossible on the shipped head**: only **75 of 333** head vertices above the brow have any other surface beneath them, so the hair is the skull's own sculpted outer surface rather than a cap over a scalp, and five different collapse rules each either tore a hole through the crown or left the fringe untouched. Removing it needs a re-authored head while maintaining or explicitly rebuilding the M004 shape and M005 garment correspondence. The [licensed long-hair audition](plans/character-mmo/results/m006-hair-audition.md) located two CC0 MakeHuman system assets and proved a tail-only ponytail on the active 65-joint bind in reviewed live motion (Telegram **796**). The full caps intersect the fused hair; the tail still needs boundary, color, helmet and shape-extreme work. The [follow-up experiments](plans/character-mmo/results/m006-source-experiments.md) reduced its diagnostic body from 7.43 to 4.00 MB with a 1024² masked PNG and reviewed live motion (Telegram **798**). A scalp cut based on texture darkness and a MakeHuman scalp graft both failed offline visual review. Cross-topology young-to-old delta transfer preserved the face but read as a changed adult identity rather than clearly older. The earlier whole-head old/bald MakeHuman fit was also rejected for a jagged neck seam, missing eyes and changed identity. Nothing was shipped and no capability flag was raised in place of the missing art.

A [new separate old/bald Human candidate](plans/character-mmo/results/m006-old-bald-candidate.md) supplies eyes and a fitted neck rim while preserving the starter head. A Blender joint-order and mesh-frame mismatch initially sent garments offscreen; the adapter now restores the active bind and the short stout candidate wears the refitted Wayfarer outfit in reviewed live motion (Telegram **801**). The optional asset is 5.88 MB after atlas reduction. Its close neck texture seam, other outfits, long-hair policy and creator state remain open, so M006 is still not complete.

The [M007 checkpoint](plans/character-mmo/results/m007.md) verifies the semantic coverage slice without claiming milestone acceptance. It enumerates all 768 catalogue combinations, validates the active body-mesh adapters against actual GLBs and makes the streamed path use semantic body visibility while preserving garment-part precedence and the provisional Undead pack. Post-change Human, Orc and Undead live checks matched every predicted body visibility state; failed and superseded requests preserved the committed appearance. Reviewed gameplay motion was delivered as Telegram **793–795** with matching 1280×720 metadata and verified [VE Human](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-human-2026-09-28.mp4), [Orc](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-orc-2026-09-28.mp4) and [Undead](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-undead-2026-09-28.mp4) playback. A paired five-route measurement stayed over 194 mean FPS at 1280×720, with 14.1 ms worst candidate interval; the local 20-run playable startup p95 was 843 ms. The [active Undead bind correction](plans/character-mmo/results/m007-undead-rebind.md) then rebuilt all eight fitted garments on their body's actual skin and was reviewed in live motion (Telegram **797**). An explicit-Undead five-route check measured 214–249 mean FPS; one bridge run tripped a cap heuristic and remains suspect. The eight garment files are 22.7% smaller in aggregate, the active 36-asset census reports zero errors, and default Human startup files and game JavaScript did not change. The M006 creator/extreme-shape and M007 close-seam gates remain open. No new release was made.

M003's native candidate is viable only as a **developer diagnostic**: 300 fully dressed mixed Human actors measured ~242 mean FPS in the flat 1280×720 probe, while 1,000 mixed actors settled near 77 mean FPS with ~31 ms p99 intervals. A VAT-to-independent same-scene switch can leave a bind pose, so the probe rejects it and compares a fresh independent page. M009 must resolve shadow tiers, state transitions and detail/upload budgets before integrating crowds. M004 preserved the verified Human source rig and bind and established shape correspondence by construction; per-instance arbitrary morphing inside a crowd batch remains unproven. The M003 probe and prepared binaries are absent from the Pages bundle, and no release was made.

## Shipped game and latest release

The active game is root `index.html` / `ashen-reach.html`, Vite **5173**, debug global **ASHEN**. Babylon Lite **1.31.1** / WebGPU and Havok **1.3.14** remain pinned. The current region is finite; source-compatible animation, equipment, spells, terrain and cathedral traversal remain the playable foundation.

Production: [play.sparkify.dev](https://play.sparkify.dev), game source **`76e3c41bf24956b85d3ea0776d571eebea6c7e91`**, Cloudflare Pages **`72fd1e3d-7d8d-4108-8f0f-d8eb9a42cbfe`**. This is not a hundreds-of-characters benchmark or a per-frame 144 FPS guarantee. Removing abandoned GPU harness pages and fixing prototype shadow stride resolved the reported regression.

Progressive startup reaches a dressed, grounded, controllable starting area while the remaining region loads behind a physical frontier. Physical iPhone startup, memory and crowd capacity are unmeasured; the user previously reported about 60 FPS on iPhone 14 Pro Max.

## Current character boundary and unresolved risks

- The [M001 parsed census](baselines/character-mmo/m001/asset-census.json) identifies active Human/Orc/Undead and startup Human geometry, bind and animation. Its finding that all eight active Undead garments had a different rest/inverse bind was corrected in the [M007 checkpoint](plans/character-mmo/results/m007-undead-rebind.md). The older MakeHuman diagnostic profile and provisional Undead pack are not active defaults.
- Existing equipment has explicit fit identities, coverage/slot occupancy, on-demand loading, shared pose and evaluated sockets. It is a limited fitted catalogue, not a general parametric wardrobe. Arbitrary body sliders, production Elf, robust semantic coverage across all races and crowd rendering remain unproven.
- Anatomy/clothing quality, extreme motion clipping, two-handed/Orc grip polish and terrain-aware feet need measured review. Old plans sometimes contain later superseded fixes; use live evidence to identify what remains.
- Static sun-shadow caching and asynchronous shader trials were negative/neutral for the measured workload; they remain disabled. See the [investigation](archive/plans/rendering-performance-investigation-2026-09-26.md). Do not re-enable them based on feature availability alone.
- The M003 seven-enemy town confirmation is blocked by VAT caster composition in the unchanged production scene. A temporary shadowless trial at 1280×720 had a no-crowd baseline of 111–112 mean FPS and is not an accepted MMO-capacity claim. Preserve load time, animation correctness and memory ownership while designing future representations.
