The [M007 extreme-motion fit result](plans/character-mmo/results/m007-extreme-motion-fit.md) closes the extreme-motion half of the fit gate with a measurement rather than a visual call. It is validated two ways: skinning at the bind pose reproduces the mesh to **1.19e-7 m**, and at that pose it reports M005's accepted rest numbers (14 newly uncovered, 42.5 mm worst gap against 14 and 42.46). The raw count needed four evidence-driven corrections — the refitted garments carry **no animation** and hang on the body's skeleton at runtime; vertices cross hems near-symmetrically as the torso turns (mean `newlyUncovered - newlyCovered` is **-3.0** across 600 rows); coverage along the 60 mm normal is not visibility (**109 of 113** flagged vertices on the worst stout row are hidden from all eight directions); and with no gloves equipped, hands are bare in every configuration, which on the worst slender row was the entire result.
