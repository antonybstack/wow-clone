can create the tall/slender ponytail and short/stout Weathered bald examples,

and delivering a second, verified content update **if the primary release is

the cost of the next armor addition. More screenshots, larger test counts and
another developer-only asset do not substitute for either result.

### Milestone 1: source fixes, with a bounded review

1. Finish the current correction's receipt, documentation, commit and push.
2. Use the ordinary route and actual `Sprint_Loop` phase to check the original
   Human. Native GPU triangle picking and component visibility identify an
   exposed surface; colour alone does not. Reuse the existing partition helper
   where appropriate. Verify original triangle union, skin/morph/bind/curves,
   bare restoration and full/compact publication independently.
3. Capture the boots close up from side, front and below at matched idle/run/
   landing poses. The current boot is one primitive per boot asset, with its
   sole connected to the upper; do not implement a fictitious separate-slab
   removal. Correct the authored sole rim/underside in the native foot frame,
   preserve skin/UV/rig interfaces, and regenerate fitted variants through the
   existing source pipeline. Compare the candidate to the same baseline.
4. Reject candidates that introduce foot exposure, ground penetration or worse
   movement. Keep the inherited sampling issue separate. A new anti-aliasing
   or rendering feature is out of scope without a measured need and benefit.

Exit requires actual reviewed movement, corrected release defects on the original
adventurer as well as the two named characters, and no newly introduced fit defect.
A confirmed shared-back hole is a release blocker. An unsuccessful candidate is retained as research evidence, not called fixed.

1. Freeze the corrected source and build once through the normal Pages workflow.

   The required p95 is <=1,000 ms in each cohort; report every miss and first-use
   GPU outlier. First play must be dressed, identity/shape-correct, grounded,

   Six profiles distinguish actual optional first-play payloads (including all
