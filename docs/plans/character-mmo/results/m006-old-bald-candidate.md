# M006 old/bald Human source candidate

Status: **rig-compatible diagnostic, not an accepted creator control** (2026-09-28). Source base `42880b1`. The default Human, startup assets and Pages bundle are unchanged.

The earlier MakeHuman old-head fit had empty eye sockets and a jagged neck gap. [`review-old-bald-head-v2.py`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/review-old-bald-head-v2.py) keeps the current torso and M004 shape keys, cuts its fused head at the neck, fits the pinned CC0 MakeHuman old head, adds the source's low-poly eye proxy, and pulls the head's lower rim onto the body's cut contour. A lower-neck weight blend uses the actual body rim weights and eases into `Head`. Its raw GLB has three meshes, one 65-joint skin, both body shape targets and all 57 clips. It is an alternate asset; the approved starter remains untouched.

The Blender export was **not fit-compatible as exported**. It reordered **64 of 65** skin joints and parented the skinned meshes under the armature's 0.01-scale `RootNode`, whereas the active Human places the mesh at the scene root. The game borrows the body pose palette for each fitted garment by joint index. In the first live check the character appeared naked even though the tunic, trousers and boots were loaded; the body's first pose matrix had a scale near **97** instead of **0.97**, and a garment appeared far offscreen. [`assemble-old-bald-candidate.mjs`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/assemble-old-bald-candidate.mjs) remaps every vertex joint index and inverse bind into the active order, verifies a 0.002 relative inverse-bind tolerance, and places the three skinned mesh nodes at the active scene-root frame. The corrected first pose-matrix scale was **0.973**, matching the established Human. This is an asset adaptation, not a runtime special case. The script cites the [glTF skin contract](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#skins) and [glTF Transform texture compression](https://gltf-transform.dev/modules/functions/functions/compressTexture).

The assembler also reduces the optional old skin atlas from 2048² to 1024² PNG and the eye atlas from 1024² to 512². The combined candidate fell from **10,064,528** raw Blender bytes to **5,884,720** bytes (**41.5%** less), while retaining two morph targets, one 65-joint skin, three meshes and 57 clips. This is still a separate, large selection payload and has no cold-start claim. The candidate stays under ignored `.cache/character-mmo/m006/`, outside the shipped starter.

I reviewed front/side/back offline renders and a [10.086-second live orbit, walk, run and jump](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m006-old-bald-stout-2026-09-28.mp4) with `humanShape=stout`, `humanHeight=0.9` and `garmentFit=refit` at 1280×720. The Wayfarer tunic, trousers, boots and sword now stay with the character; no page errors were reported. The capture had 302 frames, one out-of-order arrival corrected by timestamp sorting, square pixels and zero rotation. Telegram **801** returned matching dimensions and 10.086-second duration; VE served `video/mp4` with the 26,614,449-byte file. Telegram **800** is the earlier neutral raw candidate that predates the palette and frame correction and is retained as failure evidence, not acceptance evidence. Recording was active, so neither clip is an FPS measurement.

The close offline render still shows a sharp skin-texture line at the neck. The variant has one authored old appearance, not a continuous age control. Hair dye, long-hair boundary and headwear/cape policy, the second outfit, shape/height extremes and creator persistence remain open. No M006 capability flag or production asset was added.
