# M003 result — native crowd feasibility

Status: **complete as a bounded feasibility experiment, with production blockers** (2026-09-27 local time). Source base: `2136879` plus the M003 working tree. The [brief](../m003-crowd-feasibility.md) defines the gate. No game release or crowd renderer was deployed.

## Decision

Use Babylon Lite 1.31.1's native VAT and thin instances as the **candidate for compatible mid-distance crowd groups**. The prototype correctly groups two dressed Human appearances by concrete body/gear mesh, keeps per-actor clip phase across pieces, and draws 300 mixed actors well above 120 mean FPS in a flat diagnostic scene. It is **not ready for the production town**: enrolling even one VAT PBR mesh in Ashen Reach's custom dynamic CSM caster path causes Lite's shader composer to throw in `topoSort`. A temporary shadow-caster exclusion let the scene run but was reverted because it would alter startup provenance and needs a separate production review. The current default game and startup payload are unchanged.

The other major limit is full-detail cost. The two prepared appearances are **20,521** and **39,638** triangles per actor. At 1,000 mixed actors, the flat probe falls to about **75–77 mean FPS** after its first run, with ~31 ms p99 intervals. This result does not justify a 1,000-player or 144 FPS promise. M009 needs lower-detail representations, bounded uploads, deliberate shadow tiers and an actual-town integration gate. M004 can proceed with the established exact Human rig/bind constraints, without assuming arbitrary per-instance shape morphing works with VAT.

## What was built

- A separate [developer page](../../../../character-crowd-probe.html) and [probe](../../../../src/character/crowd-probe/main.js), excluded from the Pages entry graph. The game route does not import it.
- A [reproducible offline preparer](../../../../scripts/character-assets/prepare-crowd-probe.mjs) that merges the **active** split Human garments onto the active 65-joint body skin only after ordered joint and inverse-bind validation. It retains the current equipment visibility map and Idle/Walk clips. A rigidly weighted diagnostic sword/greatstaff follows RightHand through the native skin. These simplified props prove attachment movement, not final weapon art or exact two-hand contact.
- Two ignored developer GLBs: Wayfarer **2,425,128 bytes**, Warden **3,066,296 bytes**. Their hashes, provenance, geometry counts, item hashes and recipes are in the [prepared asset manifest](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/prepared-assets.json). Regeneration produced the same hashes. They are served only from `.cache/character-mmo/m003` by Vite development middleware; no generated GLB is in `public` or the Pages output.
- A [pure actor-to-piece batch planner](../../../../src/character/crowd-probe/batches.js). Body and boots share a batch across outfits; distinct garments/props have separate keys. Stable logical IDs map to each compact batch's indices, and phase is derived from actor identity rather than a garment array index. Native `prepareVatMany`, `createVatBakeResults`, `attachVat`, `setThinInstances` and `handle.setInstances` do the animation/instance work. The independent control loads each actor separately. VAT never attaches to a live production actor's borrowed skeleton.

## Measurement and visual evidence

M1 Max 32 GB, system Chrome 153, WebGPU/Metal, one owned uncapped headless renderer on CDP 10037, Vite 5873. Flat probe: actual **1280×720** canvas, one directional and one hemispheric light, no enemies, no recording, two-second warm-up, **three separate 12-second runs per completed cell**. The repeated appearance is Wayfarer. The mixed appearance alternates Wayfarer and Warden, so each actor remains fully dressed. All flat-cell actor centers project inside the camera viewport; this is a frustum-center count, not an occlusion count. FPS is the reciprocal of the mean `requestAnimationFrame` interval, not CPU or GPU execution time. Lite GPU timing was enabled where supported and sampled every twentieth frame; those samples are noisy and are retained as ancillary data. “Draws” below is the Lite engine's draw-submission counter, not an independently verified hardware draw count.

| Dressed cohort | Independent mean FPS, three-run range | Native VAT mean FPS, three-run range | Native p95 interval | Engine draw submissions, independent → VAT |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 repeated, idle/walk | 171.8–177.9 | 906.3–926.3 | 1.3–1.4 ms | 601 → 7 |
| 300 repeated, idle/walk | 36.0–36.9 | 366.4–366.6 | 3.0–3.1 ms | 1,801 → 7 |
| 1,000 repeated, idle/walk | Rejected at 300-actor safety budget | 124.4–155.8; settled runs ~124.5 | 15.6–16.0 ms | — → 7 |
| 100 mixed, idle/walk | 110.1–112.2 | 617.6–621.1 | 1.9 ms | 851 → 18 |
| 300 mixed, idle/walk | 21.6–22.1 | 241.8–241.9 | 4.5 ms | 2,551 → 18 |
| 1,000 mixed, idle/walk | Rejected at 300-actor safety budget | 74.9–108.1; settled runs ~76.7 | 13.5–13.7 ms | — → 18 |

The 1,000-actor tails matter: repeated p99 is about **16.2–16.4 ms** with individual intervals above **110 ms**; mixed p99 is about **29.6–31.0 ms**, with maxima **188–257 ms**. The 100/300 cells also retain p50/p95/p99/max and threshold counts in the [raw measurement directory](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/README.md). The 1,000 independent rows contain the explicit safety rejection; 300 independent actors already measured ~27–46 ms mean intervals and thousands of submissions, so allocating 1,000 duplicate skinned containers was not useful or safe. VAT preparation for one repeated source uses **5,790,720 CPU payload bytes** and one reported shared texture; two sources use **16,407,040 CPU payload bytes** and two reported textures. These are prepared-array counts, not total measured GPU or resident memory. Warm `set()` preparation was roughly 50–140 ms for VAT versus ~2–7 seconds for 100/300 independent actors; neither is a cold-start time.

Fresh flat shadow captures show a moving native shadow beside the independent control. A focused 100 mixed VAT shadow-enabled probe measured **595.7–599.6 FPS** with no page errors. Replacing cohorts while the flat shadow task is live can leave the newly installed crowd black; the probe does not claim reliable shadow churn. The production town is a separate, stronger failure: a **single** VAT actor reproduces the PBR caster `topoSort` error after a healthy seven-enemy game load. The [failed run](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/failed-town/attempt.json) and capture retain it. A temporary `if (mesh.vat) continue` in the custom caster enrollment prevented that error during an [experimental 1280×720 paired town trial](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/experimental-shadow-exclusion/town-1280.json): no-crowd 111.5–112.0, VAT 100 105.9–106.5, VAT 300 109.7–110.9, after-disposal 114.4–119.3 mean FPS. The 100-actor centers projected in frame; 270/300 did at 300. Buildings can still occlude actors. Run-order drift and an already sub-120 baseline preclude a town-capacity conclusion. **That patch was reverted and these town intervals are not accepted against the committed source.** The lower-resolution 960×540 trial is separately retained and labelled.

In the [60-second churn check](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/churn.json), a 100-actor cohort changed exactly 25 outfits, grew to 150 and shrank to 100. Whole-cohort transitions took **155–189 ms**; transition-frame p99 was **19.6 ms**, worst **34.6 ms**. Ten additional mixed VAT load/dispose cycles returned from 18 scene meshes/two owned containers to the one-mesh floor/zero owned containers every time, without runtime errors. A [canceled independent build](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/cancellation.json) rejected as canceled while a newer ten-actor VAT cohort stayed intact, then cleared to the one-mesh floor. The probe now rejects a same-scene VAT → independent control switch: live clip clocks advance but the skinned palette can visually remain in bind pose after VAT teardown. Fresh independent pages animate correctly. This state transition requires a Lite integration investigation before a production tier switch; the probe exposes it rather than displaying a silent T-pose.

I reviewed the actual [live 1280×720 MP4](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m003-motion-2026-09-27.mp4), including motion, mixed clothing, prop attachment, outfit swap, native teardown/rebuild and a **fresh-scene** independent control. Telegram message **789** returned matching **1280×720**, **12.760 s** metadata. The [capture manifest](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/capture.json) and [delivery record](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m003/delivery.json) preserve timing, dimensions and file hash; the file was encoded with 1:1 sample aspect ratio, 16:9 display ratio and no rotation tag. Telegram app inline/fullscreen playback was not inspected on this machine. Recording FPS was excluded from all benchmarks.

## Validation, ownership and remaining limits

- [Crowd/appearance tests](../../../../scripts/test-crowd-probe-contract.mjs): **12/12 passed** together. Existing equipment **47/47** and character **77/77** passed. Both Pages and full developer builds passed. Pages `ashen-reach.html` SHA-256 remains `153e61d1b07618787e3f3fde34a18318b6f424295ac94fd2f557d65df296a638`, and its main JS remains `f7ddaf7b56fec8365b5113063bc73bb0af1d9af4e63ef672c61b8ac804db8794`, matching M002. No crowd page, source bundle or derived GLB appears in the Pages output.
- The one owned browser was Chrome PID **38613**, CDP **10037**, with Vite parent **38498** / listener **38542** on **5873**. Measurement scripts reused its one tab and navigated it to `about:blank` after each check. Harness slot 7 then stopped both owned processes; neither port remained listening. The unrelated Chrome PID **3538**, CDP **9337**, had five blank pages and was left untouched.
- Exact per-instance slender/stout morph weights, material/dye variation, long hair, true armor fitting, polished socket offsets, animations beyond Idle/Walk, multi-race batches and production shadow composition remain unproven. M001 found active Undead garment inverse-bind/rest mismatch; it cannot join an exact Undead body batch yet. Orc has a distinct fit family. No physical iPhone crowd measurement was made.
- M004 should retain the exact Human source rig/bind and test neutral/slender/stout template correspondence before authoring a large wardrobe. M009 should own shadow-tier integration, safe live↔VAT transitions, lower-detail/occlusion budgets, upload scheduling, actual-town baseline investigation, and memory measurement. M003 supplies grouping keys and failure cases rather than a production renderer.
