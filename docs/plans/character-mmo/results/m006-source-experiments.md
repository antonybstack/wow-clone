# M006 head experiments and ponytail payload reduction

Status: **source experiments reviewed; creator entry gate remains open** (2026-09-28). Source base `4274aab`. These diagnostics do not alter the shipped Human, its startup pack or the Pages game.

The current Human head has hair sculpted into the same surface and texture as its face. I tested two ways to make a bald head while preserving the face. First, `inspect-human-scalp.mjs` selected the connected dark-texture component above the lower hairline. At a luminance threshold of 110 it selected **474 of 5,325** triangles. Front/side/back Blender renders show that selection cuts through an eye, cheek and jaw while leaving most of the rear hair. Texture darkness is not a hair mask on this atlas; the face has dark baked shading and the hair has lighter highlights. The selection is rejected, not a bald asset.

Second, `review-bald-scalp-graft.py` kept the existing face and grafted the licensed MakeHuman young scalp above a front-to-nape cut. It removed **564** original faces and added **2,138** source scalp faces. The rendered face identity and eyes remain, but the graft has a ragged edge across the brow and ears, leaves a dark nape fringe and has a flat single-patch skin color. It is rejected. This is more precise than the earlier whole-head swap failure: keeping the face is possible, but the seam and hairline need authored mesh/UV work, not a spatial threshold alone. Both diagnostics remain reproducible; their rendered images are under ignored `ve-capture/character-mmo/m006/`.

`review-human-age-transfer.py` tested the previously untried cross-topology age path. It maps a CC0 MakeHuman young-to-old delta from **4,368** source face vertices onto **635** active head vertices with a neck/hairline fade and a 1.5 cm displacement cap. Neutral and old front/side/back renders keep the current face and avoid the whole-head neck seam, but the result reads mainly as a shorter, changed adult face rather than a clearly older version. The source displacement before capping averaged **3.61 cm**, showing that this target includes broad identity/proportion changes, not only age detail. No age capability is advertised. A production age control needs an authored face delta and skin detail that preserves the approved identity.

The separate CC0 `ponytail01-tail` candidate has a more useful, accepted **payload** improvement. `assemble-long-hair-candidate.mjs` now uses glTF Transform's [`compressTexture`](https://gltf-transform.dev/modules/functions/functions/compressTexture) and a declared Sharp development dependency to resize its masked source atlas from 2048² to 1024² PNG. Its source UVs, MASK material, 4,042 triangles, 65-joint skin and 57 clips survive assembly. The combined diagnostic GLB fell from **7,431,280** to **4,000,944 bytes** (46.2%). The default play body still carries none of this optional asset.

I compared corresponding normal-camera frames from the previous 2048² live capture and the [new 10.16-second live orbit/walk/run/jump clip](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m006-ponytail-1024-2026-09-28.mp4) (Telegram **798**). The ponytail silhouette and alpha mask read the same at that distance; this is not a close hair-strand quality claim. The capture had 303 ordered 1280×720 frames, square pixels, no rotation, one intercepted body request and zero page errors. Telegram returned matching dimensions. VE returned `video/mp4` and the 11,669,047-byte file. The tie/back boundary, color match and dye control, hood/helmet concealment, cape contact and shape extremes remain unaccepted.

The live pass used owned slot 7, Vite **5873**, Chrome PID **69032**, CDP **10037**. Its tab and harness were stopped. User-owned Chrome PID **3538** / CDP **9337** had only `about:blank` pages and was not touched. Recording was active, so no FPS conclusion comes from the clip. No production deployment follows from these candidate diagnostics.
