# M007 mixed-equipment coverage: verified implementation checkpoint

Status: **coverage slice verified; full M007 acceptance remains open** (2026-09-28). Source base `4aabe14`. M006's creator and source-art gates are still open, so body/height extremes, adult age, and long hair under headwear cannot yet be accepted. This result preserves a measured coverage implementation without advertising the larger mixed-fit milestone as complete.

## Implemented

- `coverage-contract.js` defines race-independent body segments, seam IDs and adapters for the shipped Human, Orc and Undead body meshes. It hides a whole glTF mesh only when all segments it carries are covered. The active Human and Undead body are each fused into one mesh, so their garment coverage reports a reasoned partial-coverage exception instead of claiming to remove concealed skin. The streamed runtime now uses this resolver for body visibility while retaining garment-part and cuff precedence. The older provisional Undead pack keeps its explicit legacy geoset path.
- Wayfarer and Graveweaver proof pieces declare both legacy and semantic coverage. The equality test prevents one vocabulary drifting during migration. Orc shorts carry both waist and upper-leg segments; a waist-only translation hid them under 168 valid torso-only loadouts. The corrected adapter preserves the shipped body visibility for all valid loadouts.
- The exhaustive matrix covers **768** catalogue combinations: **672 valid**, **96 rejected** by the two-hand occupancy rule, **zero** independent-validator disagreements, **24** garment pairs and **34** three-piece combinations across the three active races. It reads body mesh names from their actual `body.glb` assets. [Matrix and exceptions](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m007/coverage-matrix.json) and `scripts/test-coverage-contract.mjs` carry the reproducible detail.

## Live and visual checks

On the owned slot 7 renderer (Chrome PID 6393, CDP 10037, Vite 5873, 1280×720), the post-change [Human](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m007/live-coverage-human.json), [Orc](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m007/live-coverage-orc.json) and [Undead](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m007/live-coverage-undead.json) probes each equipped eight representative states. Every actual `scene.meshes` membership and visibility result matched the semantic resolver. A failed request preserved the committed loadout; five concurrent torso requests settled on the final one. All three probes reported zero page errors. Slot 7 was then stopped; the separate user CDP 9337 stayed on `about:blank` and was not touched.

I reviewed front, side, back and moving frames from three **post-change** gameplay captures: [Human](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-human-2026-09-28.mp4) (Telegram **793**), [Orc](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-orc-2026-09-28.mp4) (**794**) and [Undead](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m007-undead-2026-09-28.mp4) (**795**). They show the shipped set, cross-set hood, cross-set robe/gloves, no-boots cuffs, two-handed staff, and rapid swaps while the actor turns, walks, runs and jumps. No gross missing body region or detached garment appeared at the captured normal camera distance. This does **not** resolve small seam penetration, extreme shapes, or the known Undead garment bind mismatch. Those remain explicit fit gates.

Each delivered clip is 1280×720 H.264, square pixels, about 34.4 seconds, with no rotation. `scripts/tg` probed and sent explicit dimensions; Telegram returned matching 1280×720 metadata. VE returned `video/mp4`, the exact file lengths, and HTTP 206 byte ranges; Chromium read 1280×720 metadata and advancing playback for all three. Telegram inline/fullscreen client appearance was not independently inspected in this session.

The three captures precede a recorder fix for rare out-of-order CDP frame arrivals: their concat manifests contain 7–8 intervals clamped to 1 ms each. They remain visual motion evidence, but no frame-time or animation-speed claim is drawn from those MP4s. The recorder now sorts capture timestamps, drops exact duplicates and rejects dimension changes before creating a new concat manifest. Performance was measured separately without recording.

## Performance and verification

M1 Max on AC power, macOS 26.6.2, Chrome 153, uncapped Chromium WebGPU at an actual 1280×720 buffer, seven enemies, no recording: three 12-second runs per route. A fresh `4aabe14` archive and this checkout used the same public assets and Chrome instance, one game tab at a time. The candidate is about 5% faster in mean frame interval on each route; the uniform shift is not attributed to this small coverage change. There is no measured frame-rate regression. Candidate worst per-run p99 was 11.5 ms and worst sampled frame was 14.1 ms, with no interval above 16.67 ms. The baseline had three isolated 65–69 ms intervals.
