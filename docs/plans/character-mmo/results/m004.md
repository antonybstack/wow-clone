# M004 result — canonical Human template

Status: **implemented, measured and visually reviewed** (2026-09-27 local time). Source base: `5dadafe` plus the M004 working tree; the pre-existing `AGENTS.md` edit was preserved. The [brief](../m004-human-template.md) defines the gate. No shipped character, no production asset and no deployment changed: the shape family is a developer candidate under `.cache/`, reached only through an explicit query.

## Decision

Keep the current authored Human source and **derive the template from it** by adding two morph targets to the body the game already loads. Neutral is the shipped character in every semantic accessor; slender and stout are pure displacement sets over it.

| Candidate | Topology and deformation | Licence | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reuse the current source unchanged | `HumanV1Body`, 3,274 vertices, 5,325 triangles, 65-joint bind, 57 clips. M001 verified all eight Human garments match its rest hierarchy and inverse binds. | Tripo generation from the approved reference; `blender/characters/sources/human-tripo/provenance.json` records the source but no standalone grant. Already in production. | Correct, and offers no shape variation. |
| **Template derived from it** (chosen) | Same mesh, same order, same joint centres, same inverse binds, same clips, plus `slender` and `stout` morph targets. Vertex correspondence holds by construction rather than by matching. | Adds no new third-party source. The shape magnitudes come from measurements of the CC0 MakeHuman bodies — numbers, not vertex data — so no MakeHuman geometry, bind or target file is copied into the asset. | Chosen. |
| Adopt MakeHuman hm08 as the body | 19,158-vertex CC0 mesh with a full official target library, so a parametric space arrives free. | CC0, already vendored with hashes. | Rejected. It replaces the approved character, invalidates all eight fitted garments and the compressed startup pack, and contradicts the architecture register's "preserve approved reference; no arbitrary source swap". The project already retired its MakeHuman diagnostic body. |

The residual licence question on the Tripo base is inherited, not introduced: M004 ships no new dependency and the same body is already live in production. M001 flagged it and it stays open for a decision before any *new* redistributed derivative.

## Where the shape comes from

[`measure-makehuman-girth.py`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/measure-makehuman-girth.py) builds three bodies from the vendored CC0 MakeHuman source — lean, midpoint and heavy on the weight/muscle axis — and measures, per anatomical segment, the weighted mean distance of that segment's skinned vertices from its own bone axis, split into body depth and cross-width. The output is [twenty ratio pairs](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m004/makehuman-girth.json) with the source files' hashes. Those targets move girth and not stature, so the script's stature normalisation was a measured no-op: all three bodies are 17.4813 source units tall.

| Segment | Slender width / depth | Stout width / depth |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Hips | 0.979 / 0.848 | 1.028 / 1.168 |
| Spine1 | 0.832 / 0.848 | 1.175 / 1.164 |
| Neck | 0.840 / 0.812 | 1.168 / 1.250 |
| Shoulder | 0.744 / 0.765 | 1.273 / 1.283 |
| Upper arm | 0.728 / 0.733 | 1.354 / 1.349 |
| Upper leg | 0.747 / 0.831 | 1.288 / 1.195 |
| Head | 0.998 / 0.999 | 1.003 / 1.001 |
| Foot | 0.999 / 0.996 | 1.001 / 1.004 |

[`build-human-shape-family.mjs`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/build-human-shape-family.mjs) applies those ratios on our own mesh in bone space. For each vertex and each joint that skins it, the offset from that joint's rest axis is split into the same two perpendicular axes and scaled; the per-joint results are blended with the mesh's existing skin weights. Using the skin weights as the blend makes the field continuous wherever the deformation already is, with no seam handling, and gives exactly zero to the 45 joints with no measured segment — fingers, toes, eyes, head tip — so hand grip and foot contact are untouched. Scaling only the perpendicular components leaves every joint centre, every bone length and every inverse bind exactly where they were.

This is a girth field, not an anatomical resculpt. It widens and narrows segments; it does not redistribute mass within one.

## Height

`player.setHeightScale` already existed, unused, clamping to **[0.9, 1.15]** of the profile's 1.748 m capsule and reshaping the Havok controller through `setShapeOptions`. M004 reuses that clamp instead of introducing a second one, so a visual height can never outrun what physics accepts. Height is applied before the body is attached, so the body root and the sockets mount against the already-scaled capsule; the camera pivot is the one value not derived from the body, and is scaled explicitly.

## What the shape family does to the existing garments

The active Human is a single mesh with no hideable regions, so a tunic works only because it is authored to sit outside the body's surface. [`measure-shape-garment-fit.mjs`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/measure-shape-garment-fit.mjs) spends that margin and counts the result: a body vertex is covered when a ray along its own outward normal finds cloth, and the headline is the change against the neutral body, which is the control.

| Garment | Covered on neutral | Newly uncovered on slender | Newly uncovered on stout |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| wayfarerTunic | 913 | 43 | **364** |
| wayfarerTrousers | 756 | 11 | **250** |
| wayfarerBoots | 669 | 0 | 9 |

Stout pushes 40% of the tunic's covered surface and 33% of the trousers' outside the cloth, with a worst gap of 30.2 mm — visible in the delivered clip as skin through cloth at the shoulders and thighs. Slender fails the other way: the body recedes, and 43 vertices end up more than the 60 mm ray length from the tunic, so the cloth floats. Both are M005's target, in numbers rather than adjectives.

Two earlier versions of this measurement were discarded because they failed their own control. Signing each vertex against its nearest garment triangle reported 72 penetrations on the shipped, visibly correct neutral body, because these garments are shelled and the inner surface's normal points back at the body. Treating every uncovered vertex within 60 mm as a penetration reported 174, because most of those are simply outside the garment's area. Rest pose only; animation is not evaluated, so a clean rest-pose result would not by itself prove a clean walk cycle.

## An engine defect found and worked around

Lite 1.31.1's glTF morph feature resolves each morph target accessor with `resolveAccessor`, which builds a tightly packed typed array from the buffer view offset and **ignores `bufferView.byteStride`** (`node_modules/@babylonjs/lite/lib/loader-gltf/gltf-parser.js`). glTF Transform interleaves vertex data by default, which is legal glTF: POSITION at offset 0 and NORMAL at offset 12 in a stride-24 view. Lite then read that as one run of positions, and the character rendered as shattered triangles in every shaped case while neutral — which loads the shipped body instead — was fine.

The smallest reproduction is any skinned glTF whose morph target accessors share an interleaved buffer view. The workaround is one line, `setVertexLayout(VertexLayout.SEPARATE)`, and `scripts/test-human-shape-contract.mjs` now fails if a morph accessor is ever written interleaved again. Lite was not upgraded and no engine file was patched.

## Verification

### Live behaviour in the real route

[`check-human-shape-live.mjs`](../../../../scripts/character-assets/check-human-shape-live.mjs) ran six cases through full page loads on the owned harness slot at an actual 1280×720 buffer with seven enemies: neutral, slender, stout, a 0.5/0.5 blend, slender at 0.90 and stout at 1.15. Every case asserted Havok driving the character, the GPU morph weights matching the request, the capsule and the visual root scale matching the requested height, the camera pivot at 0.55 × scale, the main-hand socket resolved, and real travel under a held W key. Results are in [`live-shape-check.json`](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m004/live-shape-check.json); travel ranged 5.42–5.77 m per case and no page or console error appeared in any of them.

That last point is the one worth naming. M003 found that enrolling a single native baked-animation mesh in Ashen Reach's custom cascaded-shadow caster path made Lite's shader composer throw in `topoSort`, and that blocker is still open. A morphed *skinned* PBR body composes cleanly in the same scene: Lite inserts the morph deltas into the vertex stage's `VR` slot before skinning rather than replacing the skinning path, so the caster shader it composes is the one the town already builds.

## Limits and inputs for M005

- **Garments do not follow the shape.** The numbers above are M005's target. Boots are nearly unaffected; the tunic and trousers are not.
- **Girth only.** The field widens and narrows segments about their bone axes. It does not redistribute mass within a segment, does not move joint centres, and does not change bone lengths, so it cannot express a long-limbed or short-limbed build. Those need a retarget with new inverse binds and contact validation.
- **Tangents are not morphed.** Lite's glTF loader reads POSITION and NORMAL targets only, so the base tangent frame is kept. At these displacements the normal-map orientation error is small, and it was not measured.
- **Face, age, hair, dyes: none.** The head girth ratio is 1.00 by measurement, so the head is untouched by design. M006 owns those controls and the M002 schema migration they need.
- **Race switching under an active shape is untested.** `switchRace` replaces the body, which has no morph targets for Orc or Undead and would reset the visual root scale. The shape family is Human-only; M008 owns race adapters.
- **Crowd compatibility is unproven.** Morph weights are per mesh and Lite's baked-animation path drops the live skeleton when it attaches. M003's blocker is still open and M009 owns both questions.
- **Animation was not evaluated for garment fit.** The fit measurement is rest pose only.
- **Licence.** The Tripo base carries no recorded standalone grant. Inherited from production, unchanged by M004, and still open before any new redistributed derivative.
