# M006 neck and headwear checkpoint

Status: **optional diagnostic assets; M006 is still open** (2026-09-28). The default Human, starter pack and production asset set were not replaced or released.

The CC0 ponytail candidate now attaches to the **M004 shape-family body**, retaining the two body morph targets, 65-joint bind and 57 clips. The assembled `human-ponytail01-tail-shape-family-candidate.glb` is 4,768,560 bytes (SHA-256 `8c9265c9a7842314355422ceb62c8b7d06b6471319e590a4c59756b0426f0395`) under ignored `.cache/character-mmo/m006/`. Its separate `HumanPonytail01` mesh carries `head.scalp` in a pack-specific semantic adapter. The existing hood hides only that mesh; the fused Human face stays visible. This follows the [glTF mesh visibility boundary](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#meshes) and reuses M007 coverage instead of adding a second headwear system. No cape item is in the current catalogue, so cape interaction remains unverified.

In the live 1280×720 renderer, neutral, slender and stout all passed tail visible → Graveweaver hood hides tail → remove hood restores tail, with no page errors. The short stout (height 0.9) and tall slender (height 1.15) endpoints passed the same check with Havok capsule heights 1.5732 and 2.0102 m. Switching to Orc and back restored the separate tail and the refitted Wayfarer tunic/trousers/boots at the correct shape weights. The return exposed a real runtime gap: newly streamed garments began at neutral weight while the parked body retained its shape. `switchRace` now reapplies the Human weights after the new stream settles. The explicit `?humanHair=ponytail&humanShape=stout&garmentFit=refit` route is developer-only and refuses a shaped candidate with shipped, unfitted garments.

Reviewed [live motion](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m006-ponytail-shape-headwear-2026-09-28.mp4) covers orbit, walk, run, jump, the hood transition and return. Telegram **802** returned 1280×720 and 14.091 seconds; VE served `video/mp4` with 25,444,031 bytes and HTTP 206 byte range. The tail reads at ordinary camera distance, though its rear tie and color boundary still need final art review before becoming a creator choice.

The separate old/bald head had another shape-specific defect. At the neck cut the M004 body moves roughly 12–14 mm, while the old head previously had no morph targets. A stout live inspection showed a dark gap under the chin. The assembler now copies the body's two neck displacement fields onto the old head, tapering to zero over the first 9 cm so the authored older face stays unchanged. It verifies the active 65-entry rest palette after Blender's joint-order and mesh-frame repair. The new optional `human-old-bald-candidate.glb` is 6,090,640 bytes (SHA-256 `fd29e10709598b21a2caadecc84469105060358af553f5a7434a605bdf715b0a`), 205,920 bytes above the earlier candidate. The old stout rim's median nearest-vertex distance after morph fell from 15.8 mm to 5.2 mm; this metric includes different rim vertex spacing and does not prove a welded seam. A 2.5 cm underlap produced visible jagged strips and was reverted. Normal blending and a local tint did not remove the seam in offline/live review; those experiments were also left out of the candidate.

Reviewed [old/bald live motion](https://ve.sparkify.dev/wow-clone/ashen-reach/character-mmo/m006-old-bald-neck-morph-2026-09-28.mp4) shows the short stout actor walking, running, jumping and orbiting bare-neck in the inspection camera. The dark front gap is removed; a bright skin-color line is still visible at the neck from the side/back and bare front. Telegram **803** returned 1280×720 and 13.861 seconds; VE served `video/mp4` with 28,928,835 bytes and HTTP 206 byte range. This is a geometry/pose correction, **not visual acceptance** of the old head.

Performance was measured separately from recording on M1 Max, uncapped Chromium WebGPU, 1280×720, seven enemies, town route, three 12-second runs per sequence, one active game renderer. Baseline A measured 182.7–186.9 mean FPS, ponytail candidate 203.9–208.6, baseline B 183.2–186.6. The candidate adds roughly four draw submissions and its GPU mean was 1.09–1.13 ms versus 1.05–1.13 ms for baseline. All six baseline and three candidate runs stayed above 144 mean FPS; no sampled interval exceeded 16.7 ms (worst baseline 13.4 ms, candidate 11.7 ms). The candidate repackages the body as well as adding hair, and baseline repeated at its original speed, so the higher candidate CPU throughput is **not evidence that hair improves performance** or an isolated hair-cost measurement. Raw reports are in [`docs/baselines/character-mmo/m006/`](../../../baselines/character-mmo/m006/).

The remaining M006 gates are a coherent young/old head family with a closed color/material seam, hair and skin color controls that do not tint unrelated surfaces, final long-hair cap/helmet/cape behavior, second-outfit and motion-extreme fit review, and a creator with versioned local persistence, undo/reset, race filtering and gameplay round-trip. Only height/body shape and this headwear rule are currently proven; neither the old/bald nor the tail candidate has been added to the M002 appearance capability registry or production assets.
