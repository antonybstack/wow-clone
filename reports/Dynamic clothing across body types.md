# Author Orc garments, hide covered skin

**Do not wrap Human clothes onto the print-sculpt Orc at runtime.** Keep the existing two-pack catalogue (`public/ashen-reach/equipment/` and `equipment-orc/`), the shared 65-joint Mixamo bind, and Lite linear-blend skinning, then **replace the Orc rest meshes** so Wayfarer boots enclose the print foot and calf and Graveweaver gloves enclose the print hand and forearm, and **split `OrcV1Body`** into the same six coverage regions Human already hides. That is the documented MMO/RPG pattern for discrete bodies with a small wardrobe: Baldur’s Gate 3 tells modders to refit each race and hide covered skin with vertex-colour masks ([Armor Creation Guidelines](https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/r/creation-guidelines-armor)); World of Warcraft ships a separate `orcmale.m2` with geosets, not a Human Male morpher ([wowdev Character Customization](https://wowdev.wiki/index.php?title=Character_Customization&oldid=34753)); Capcom authored male and female Monster Hunter armor as separate files through World and Rise ([PC Gamer / Capcom, 2024](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/fashion-hunters-rejoice-as-monster-hunter-wilds-ditches-gendered-armor-capcom-confirms-all-characters-can-wear-any-gear/)). The three screenshots are rest-pose coverage failures from `fit-orc-sculpt-clothes.py` (uniform stature scale **2.10 / 1.80 = 1.1667**, then a 1.0 cm / 8 mm BVH push-off) plus `packVisibility` forcing `OrcV1Body` always on. A runtime wrap, MHCLO-on-print, Human→Orc blendshape bank, cloth-capsule sim, or texture-compositing bake will not grow a Human last around an Orc toe, and would spend frame time this slice does not have. Completely refactoring into a “dynamic to any skeleton and mass” engine is how the current bug was produced; reject it.

## Uniform scale plus nearest-surface push leaves a Human last on an Orc foot

The live Orc pack already streams the same eight logical items as Human and borrows the 65-joint GPU palette. What it does **not** do is grade per limb, enclose foot volume, lengthen gloves, or hide covered print skin. Human Wayfarer boots rest from about **Y −0.010 to 0.360 m**; after ×1.17 they top out near **0.42 m**. Human Graveweaver gloves span **9.2 cm**; after scale and push they sit about **16 cm at the wrist**. `OrcV1Body` is one surface. `packVisibility` in `equipment-stream.js` maps only `OrcV1Hair ← HumanHair` and `OrcV1Shorts ← BodyUnderLegs`, then sets `OrcV1Body = true`. Boots declare `coverage: ['BodyUnderBoots']` and gloves `['BodyHands']` in `equipment-catalog.js`, but those names do not exist on the sculpt body, so toes, calves, and forearms stay drawn under undersized leather.

That is exactly what Blender Shrinkwrap is specified to do. Nearest Surface Point “will select the nearest point over the surface of the shrunk target”; Offset is a stay-off-surface distance, not a coverage scale; vertices whose Project rays miss the target “are left in their original position” ([Blender Shrinkwrap Modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/shrinkwrap.html)). A Human toe-cap vertex that sat a few centimetres in front of a Human toe finds the Orc **instep or the side of the big toe**, not the new tip, because assignment is closest-from-current-garment, not “cover this body region.” Solidify then extrudes local thickness and “does not” extend coverage along a limb ([Blender Solidify](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/solidify.html)). MakeClothes authors already warn that unconstrained proximity matching parks trumpet sleeves on the torso “because the torso vertices are nearer to the sleeves than the arm” ([MakeClothes Vertex-Groups](http://www.makehumancommunity.org/wiki/Documentation:MakeClothes_Vertex-Groups)). Shot 1 (toes out) is a last that never grew. Shot 2 (boot on the lower shin, calf bare) is a shaft whose rest vertices already lived near a Human ankle. Shot 3 (thin sleeve on a massive forearm) is Toigo’s short glove, still short, with no `BodyHands` hide.

MHCLO would have scaled the leftover offset by a **Foot** or **Hand** landmark pair (`x_scale v1 v2 dist` in the header; runtime `w1*r1 + w2*r2 + w3*r3 + (s1*d1, s2*d2, s3*d3)`) ([MakeHuman file formats](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/oldsite/documentation/file_formats_and_extensions.html)). That operator needs **stable hm08 body indices**. The print-sculpt has none of those vertex IDs, so the Human MHCLO cannot map it. Weight transfer is the same class of miss: Blender’s Nearest Face Interpolated copies bone weights, not rest extent ([Blender Data Transfer](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html)). Copying Orc foot weights onto a Human-sized boot still yields a Human-sized boot that deforms with the Orc ankle. Unreal Leader Pose and Unity `SkinnedMeshRenderer.bones` remap are the engine version of that miss: they share the leader’s bone-transform buffer; extra or skipped joints render in reference pose; they do not wrap armor onto a different body ([Working with Modular Characters](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/working-with-modular-characters-in-unreal-engine)). Lite already shares one evaluated Mixamo pose across garment meshes. Pose sharing is not the bug, and more of it will not bulk the mail.

Methods that **can** cover a huge foot are volumetric or pattern-space graders used **offline**: a Mesh Deform cage whose foot lobe grows (harmonic coordinates, non-negative interior falloff) ([Joshi et al., SIGGRAPH 2007](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1276377.1276466); [Blender Mesh Deform](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/mesh_deform.html)); Green Coordinates if the shaft must not shear ([Green Coordinates](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~ylipman/GC/gc_techrep.pdf)); RBF on heel/toe/ankle/knee landmarks; Marvelous Auto Fitting, which grades **2D patterns** then re-simulates ([Marvelous Auto Fitting](https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358335130649-Auto-Fitting)); Pixar GarmentRefit, which restores a tightness gap after affine-invariant relaxation rather than collapsing to the surface ([Garment Refitting for Digital Characters](https://graphics.pixar.com/library/GarmentRefit/)); MetaHuman sized outfit sources, which interpolate pre-authored (garment, body) pairs then copy weights ([Tailoring Your Own Wardrobe Items](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/tailoring-your-own-wardrobe-items?lang=en-US)). Surface Deform / Maya Wrap only ride an Orc-shaped driver if that driver **morphs from the Human topology the clothes were bound to** ([Blender Surface Deform](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/surface_deform.html)). Binding Human clothes to an already-Orc mesh is proximity assignment again. None of these operators exist in glTF. A WebGPU character is linear blend skinning of a rest `POSITION` buffer by `JOINTS_n` / `WEIGHTS_n` and inverse bind matrices; glTF “deliberately does not retain 3D authoring information” ([glTF 2.0 skins](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#skins)). Coverage has to be in the exported rest mesh. Babylon.js does not even ship dual-quaternion volume preserve ([Babylon.js forum](https://forum.babylonjs.com/t/dual-quaternion-skinning/10595)).

## Discrete bodies get discrete armour; hide what the leather covers

Larian refused sliders because millimetre face changes “can make or break a face completely,” then paid for discrete body types and **a unique armor mesh per race/body type** ([Game Developer, 2023](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/art/building-an-inclusive-character-creator-for-the-fantasy-world-of-baldur-s-gate-3)). Official toolkit copy is unambiguous: “If you want the armour to be available for other races, you’ll need to adjust it for each of those races individually.” Trousers and sleeves stay **inside** an orange width limit; gloves and boots stay **outside** it “snugly around it,” or mix-and-match slots intersect. Green vertex colour marks regions that will be hidden. Gloves must reach a length disc or there are gaps. Topology of hidden joints is still authored, or it “will pop out of the overlying model when animated” ([Armor Creation Guidelines](https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/r/creation-guidelines-armor)). Runtime hide is VertCut plus `VertexColorMaskSlots` (Shoulders, Upperarm, Torso, hands, feet, Sleeves, Pants — on the order of **twenty named slots**, not hundreds) ([Adding Armour](https://docs.baldursgate3.game/Adding_Armour); [VertexColorMaskSlots](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Modding:VertexColorMaskSlots)). Divinity-era docs required “8 meshes + 8 skeleton version variation” and hid unequippable naked parts when armor equipped ([My first: Character](https://docs.larian.game/My_first:_Character)). Community Outfit Builder shapekeys refit in Blender; they are not the runtime ([Modding:Meshes](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Modding:Meshes_(Blender))).

WoW is the same architecture with cheaper 3D. Each race×gender is its own character M2 (`character/orc/male/orcmale.m2` vs `humanmale.m2`) ([ModelDisplayPath.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Resike/BlizzardInterfaceResources/master/Resources/Data/ModelDisplayPath.lua)). Geosets already in that mesh (gloves 04**, boots 05**, sleeves, legs, robe, cape, torso — **tens of groups with a handful of variants**, not hundreds of vertex-shader zones) turn on from `ItemDisplayInfo`; most chest/leg/boot/glove look is **texture pasted** into about **ten** UV sections (ARM_UPPER 256×128, FOOT 256×64, …) on that race’s body, while helms and shoulders are attached M2s, often deformed from a Human Male hero piece ([wowdev Character Customization](https://wowdev.wiki/index.php?title=Character_Customization&oldid=34753); [ItemDisplayInfo](https://wowdev.wiki/index.php?title=DB/ItemDisplayInfo&oldid=30012); [whoahq texture pipeline](https://deepwiki.com/whoahq/whoa/5.2-character-texture-pipeline); [Ze Workshop, 2018](https://zeworkshop.wordpress.com/2018/10/24/recreating-world-of-warcrafts-character-textures-in-unity/)). Shadowlands rewrote customization compositing in **CPU SIMD** because unique baked textures had hit “millions” of permutations; that is albedo assembly, not a cloth wrap, and not a change to per-race bodies ([Engineer’s Workshop, 2020](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23564386/engineers-workshop-enhancing-character-customization)). Collection-style later pieces share the character skeleton by bone remapping of an **already-authored** mesh ([wow.export CharacterExporter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kruithne/wow.export/main/src/js/3D/exporters/CharacterExporter.js)).

FFXIV is the closest documented analog to “reuse Human Male,” and it still **does not** blendshape Hyur into Lalafell. CEDEC 2010 solved mix clipping with polygon show/hide, shape deformation, cut planes, and display priority, **recomputed when equipment changes** ([4Gamer CEDEC 2010](https://www.4gamer.net/games/092/G009287/20100901009/)). Modding docs describe four families and **runtime bone scale** from Midlander Male (`c0101`) when a racial model is missing; “You cannot scale Hyur gear down for a Lalafell. The proportions are too different” ([Barnes](https://medium.com/@danielzbarnes/ffxiv-modding-insights-to-modding-with-blender-c10c45e817c5); [XIVGuide](https://github-wiki-see.page/m/rgd87/XIVGuide/wiki/Exporting-from-TexTools)). Hide is a compact attribute set (`atr_hij` wrist, `atr_sne` shin, …) plus cuff shape keys (`shp_kat` / `shp_ude` / `shp_hij`) ([xivmodding attributes](https://xivmodding.com/books/ff14-asset-reference-document/page/attribute-reference-tables)). Viera and Hrothgar went years without most hats; Yoshida’s team “managed most of them, except for metal pieces like helmets” in 2025 — the public cost of not auto-fitting unique heads ([PC Gamer, 2025](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-14-is-doing-what-it-should-have-done-6-years-ago-and-fulfilling-its-promise-to-let-every-race-wear-hats/)). Print-sculpt Orc vs MakeHuman Human is closer to **Roe/Lala** than to Elezen-from-Midlander. Bone-scale of the current Human last will not cover the print toes.

The rest of the named games repeat the same two levers. Guild Wars 2 authors armor per race (Human/Norn/Sylvari share a frame; **Charr and Asura each require their own**, “4 meshes for each armor”) and **replaces** the upper body rather than wrapping a coat; mixing weight classes leaves holes because cutoffs differ ([ArenaNet, 2011](https://web.archive.org/web/20110212143639/http://www.arena.net/blog/designing-humans); [r/Guildwars2, 2013](https://www.reddit.com/r/Guildwars2/comments/1ivksm/follow_charr_armor_issues_with_asura_armor_issues/); [GW2 forums, 2019](https://en-forum.guildwars2.com/topic/61406-free-for-all/)). Dye is up to four material channels, including optional per-race overrides, not a mesh warp ([GW2 Wiki Dye](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dye)). Monster Hunter World stores `f_equip` vs `m_equip`; Nexus guides **rename** folders to mirror genders, which is proof the client is not wrapping one mesh onto the other ([Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/monsterhunterworld/articles/482)). Wilds unlocks both designs for any hunter; Capcom still described previous games as separate male and female armor ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/fashion-hunters-rejoice-as-monster-hunter-wilds-ditches-gendered-armor-capcom-confirms-all-characters-can-wear-any-gear/)). Elden Ring ships Type A/B meshes, unit-size armor anyone can wear, and `invisibleFlag*` masks; sliders scale the body and the skinned armor rides along, which is why extreme chests clip — there is no per-slider unique armor mesh ([Eurogamer.de](https://www.eurogamer.de/elden-ring-koerper-aendern-kopf-brust-muskeln-bauch-arme-beine-behaarung); [EquipParamProtector](https://soulsmodding.com/doku.php?id=bb-refmat:param:equipparamprotector)). Destiny Mashup places, rotates, and **scales bits in the DCC**; wrap deformers “worked very well” for cloth and soft organics and were **not** the general female-armor path; clipping was caught in Maya preview (“Mantini”), not deleted by a runtime under-mesh solver ([GDC talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sSSMC4O6J8Q); [Polygon, 2014](http://www.polygon.com/2014/3/21/5533684/bungies-destiny-extensive-character-customization)). Conan Exiles deletes hidden body polygons, attaches the remainder to the armor nif, swaps `_tucked` long gloves/boots, and converts male→female with an **offline** lattice ([Funcom primer](https://www.conanexiles.com/wp-content/wiki/2693791979.html)). ESO broke the body into **eight** parts plus about six clothing bits, copied weights and normals in Max, and targeted one draw call / 77 bones for 200-player PvP ([GDC 2015](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5YBJaXHFoSA)). Star Citizen zone-culls **about 20 to 40** regions, clothing hiding body and armor hiding clothing ([CitizenCon 2948](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQEQmO9uX28)). Kingdom Come bakes layer morphs **once** when the character changes: “there’s no run time applying” ([KCD talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ceX237jpqLs)). Roblox’s cage-to-cage patent is the product class that **does** fit arbitrary UGC bodies at runtime, which Ashen is not ([US 2022/0292791](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20220292791A1)).

The paste that attributes a single “runtime wrap / morph-from-Human-Male / hundreds of body zones / real-time collision deformers” stack to WoW, FFXIV, GW2, BDO, MHW, Destiny, and BG3 is inverted. Those MMOs kept per-race or per-body-family authored assets; their automation is texture paste, geoset tables, and joint-scale **among similar skeletons**. BDO outfits are class-locked authored meshes that hide combat gear ([BDO wiki outfits](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/Wiki?wikiNo=395)). Skyrim BodySlide Conform All is an **offline bake** against a CBBE reference ([Nexus BodySlide](https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/43642?tab=posts)). Cloth capsules are cape/skirt sim against a short list of spheres and capsules, one-way, not a boot grader ([Unity Cloth](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/class-Cloth.html)). Epic’s Chaos cloth notes are performance guidance for that sim, not an MMO armor fitter ([Chaos performance playbook](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/learning/tutorials/KWeX/unreal-engine-a-tech-artists-playbook-for-chaos-performance)).

**Reject “completely refactor into a runtime dynamic-to-any-skeleton system.”** The skeleton is already shared. Mass is a rest-pose authoring problem. Leader Pose on a Human boot, runtime weight copy, and nearest-surface wrap all leave Human rest offsets on Orc bones — which is the screenshot. A generalized engine is how you get shrink-wrapped Human mittens on an Orc while adding cloth sim, zone atlases, texture bakes, and morph banks this catalogue, crowd, and slider set do not justify. FFXIV bone-scale among **similar** races is the honest upper bound of “dynamic,” and Square still authors unique meshes when proportions break. Undead later repeats this minimum (eight rest meshes + the same hide flags), the way FFXIV added Lalafell and BG3 added Gith.

## Bake coverage in Blender, then only LBS at runtime

Minimum viable pipeline, in order. Boots first (shots 1–2), gloves second (shot 3), then the remaining six, then hood/tusk clearance. Do not restore `fit-orc-clothes.py` (MakeHuman index map). Do not run MHCLO against the print.

1. **Lock the runtime contract.** Keep item IDs, `ORC_EQUIPMENT_FIT`, streamed `public/ashen-reach/equipment-orc/{item}.glb`, 65 `mixamorig:*` joints, four influences, identity inverse binds from `equipment-orc/body.glb`, and Lite palette borrow in `equipment-stream.js`. No second skeleton, no wrap compute pass, no morph-from-Human.

2. **Partition `OrcV1Body` in DCC.** Split the one print surface into the six Human names in `BODY_REGIONS` (`BodyExposed`, `BodyUnderTunic`, `BodyUnderBoots`, `BodyUnderLegs`, `BodyWaist`, `BodyHands`), preserving every triangle once, analogous to `prepare-equipment.mjs` on Human (Y bands plus hand-weight > 0.75 for `BodyHands`). Keep `OrcV1Hair`, `OrcV1Brows`, `OrcV1Eyes`, `OrcV1Shorts`. Export that split with `bind-source-orc.mjs` so the playable body GLB carries the geosets. Covered topology stays in the file so hidden verts cannot pop from a missing bind ([BG3 topology rule](https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/r/creation-guidelines-armor)).

3. **Replace the Orc fitter’s source of truth.** `scripts/ashen-reach/fit-orc-sculpt-clothes.py` may keep uniform ×1.1667 + BVH push-off as a **starting cage only**. After that, **grade coverage per limb**. For boots, landmark-scale the last using heel, toe tip, ankle, and calf (MakeClothes Foot X1/X2 Y1/Y2 Z1/Y2 is the cheap authored version of this ([MakeClothes offset scaling](http://www.makehumancommunity.org/wiki/Documentation:MHBlenderTools:MakeClothes))). Extrude the shaft up the print calf until rest Y covers the same region `BodyUnderBoots` will hide — Human 0.36 m is the wrong target. For gloves, extend the cuff up the print forearm until it is a gauntlet, not Toigo’s 9.2 cm wristband. Prefer a **volumetric** first guess: Mesh Deform cage scaled in the foot/hand lobe, Green Coordinates if the shaft shears, or RBF on those landmarks. Do not nearest-snap and call it fitted. Marvelous pattern-grade is optional for cloth tunics; it is overkill for eight low-poly pieces if an artist can extend loops.

4. **Author mix-slot clearance.** Trouser cuffs stay inside a boot limitation hull; sleeves stay inside a glove hull; green/hidden overlap at the seam ([BG3 limitation volumes](https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/r/creation-guidelines-armor)). Keep the existing Human cuff-split at y ≈ 0.31 m as the Orc analogue once the Orc boot shaft is tall enough. Hood: sculpt tusk clearance; do not wrap the Human hood onto the muzzle.

5. **Bind weights from the Orc Mixamo skin, rest pose only.** Data Transfer **Nearest Face Interpolated** from `OrcV1Body` (not Topology, not Human weights). Max Distance so fingers do not steal forearm verts ([Blender Data Transfer](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html)). Collapse to four influences, normalize, keep `mixamorig:*` names. Apply Armature / Mesh Deform / Surface Deform **before export**. Dual-quaternion “Preserve Volume” will not survive glTF.

6. **Export rest that already covers.** Each Orc garment GLB must contain fitted `POSITION` / `NORMAL`, `JOINTS_0` / `WEIGHTS_0`, and the shared inverse bind matrices. Optional later: a same-topology morph on **that Orc mesh** for calf-loosen or hood-open. Run `prepare-orc-equipment.mjs` so joint order matches `body.glb`.

7. **Wire hide on the Orc pack.** Change `packVisibility` so Orc uses the same coverage flags as Human: boots hide `BodyUnderBoots`, gloves hide `BodyHands`, tunics hide `BodyUnderTunic` + `BodyWaist`, trousers hide `BodyUnderLegs` + `BodyWaist`, hood hides `OrcV1Hair`. Stop `out.OrcV1Body = true`. Point `ORC_BASE_VISIBLE_MESHES` at the six partitions plus hair/brows/eyes/shorts. Update `check-orc-equipment.mjs` (today it asserts the body stays visible under trousers).

8. **Acceptance is live coverage, not a signed-distance log.** Offline: boot rest AABB contains print toes and the boot shaft overlaps the print calf; glove rest contains print hand **and** forearm; no T-pose poke-through on opaque regions. Live on Vite 5173 / CDP 9337, `ashen-reach.html?play&clean`, Wayfarer and Graveweaver, idle/walk/run/jump, front/side/down: no toes through leather, no bare calf above a shin-cap, glove reads as a gauntlet, covered skin not drawn. Measure FPS without recording at 960×540; keep the >120 goal (current dressed Orc ~144 FPS, 46 draws). Telegram a reviewed live GIF/MP4; stills are not the cycle.

9. **Stop conditions.** Do not add Chaos-class cloth, a 10-section CPU/GPU texture composite, mesh merge on armory close, hundreds of hide zones, or a WGSL cage. If a third race arrives, duplicate this pack; do not invent a universal fitter first.

## Conclusion

The Orc clothing bug is not “Lite cannot do MMO armor.” It is that Ashen already cloned the cheap half of the WoW/BG3 contract (shared bind, streamed slots, Human geosets) and then used the **one operator those pipelines refuse for a second body**: nearest-surface push of a Human rest. Coverage lives in rest `POSITION`; hide lives in a handful of named partitions; dyes, cages, and capsules are other products’ permutation and cloth problems. Treating wrap as an authoring aid and freezing eight Orc GLBs is not a downgrade from AAA — it is what Larian, Capcom, and Blizzard shipped when the second body was real. The next execution step is geometric: a print-sized last and gauntlet in Blender, then `packVisibility` that can finally turn the feet off.
